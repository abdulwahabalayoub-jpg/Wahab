Evaluate organic content and paid advertising separately. Combine them only at the final overall-grade stage.
CONFIGURABLE DEMO
Why are these separate?
Organic content measures how the content performs without paid distribution. Paid advertising measures how efficiently the budget delivers the campaign objective. Organic content gets its own quality grade. Paid campaigns are graded by their chosen objective: Sales, Engagement or Traffic. We do not add a generic paid-engagement score to every campaign. Ad spend is context, not a score to reward for being high.

Rules that keep grades fair

OrganicShares, saves, organic reach, organic engagement
PaidCampaign-specific: Sales, Engagement or Traffic
Context onlyAd spend and budget
Unavailable dataExcluded, never scored as zero

ROAS and cost per result require reliable conversion tracking. Paid campaigns use objective-specific KPIs and target-achievement percentages. Profile visit and follow conversion rates belong in the organic account/content scorecard when attribution data is available. Video watch time, retention and skip rate are organic metrics in this grading setup. Account-only metrics such as follower growth are not offered in a post formula.

Data integrity: These rows are fictional examples. In production, the system should join the Instagram media ID to the corresponding boosted/promoted ad records. Reach and impressions are different metrics; paid impressions must not be mislabeled as paid reach. If Meta does not provide a comparable post-level paid reach field for a particular format or ad setup, show “Unavailable” rather than estimate it. Organic and paid unique reach may overlap and should not be blindly added to claim deduplicated total reach.
