# Microblogger Dashboard film V2: narration

1. Every campaign used to start the same way. Hundreds of DMs... and one very tired marketing team.
2. We'd find bloggers in our messages, chase them for replies, copy every detail into a spreadsheet, then start all over again for the next restaurant.
3. Or we'd pay an agency. One thousand five hundred dinars, for a hundred bloggers. And that's just the fee. Every blogger still dines with us. A forty dinar table, at seventy percent off, is twelve dinars each. That's another one thousand two hundred dinars in food. Two thousand seven hundred dinars. For one campaign.
4. So we built our own.
5. Now, when a blogger messages us, we send one link. They apply themselves. Their details land straight in our dashboard, and we approve them in one tap. Our network grows every single day. We own it, and we use it for every brand, and every campaign.
6. Create a campaign for any restaurant, choose your bloggers, and the invitations go out on WhatsApp.
7. Bloggers pick their own date and time, get confirmed instantly, and get a reminder the day before. No calls. No chasing.
8. Every booking. Every visit. Every no-show. Tracked, with reports for every brand.
9. No more agency fees. Just one network that keeps growing. This is the Microblogger Dashboard.
